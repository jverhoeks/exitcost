How we calculate

  • List prices, on-demand, excluding VAT, no discounts, reserved instances or savings plans. The current-cloud run cost uses .
  • Egress tiers are applied as if all data leaves in one month. Spread over several months, the free allowance repeats and the first, most expensive tier applies again each month.
  • Faster ways out (checked 26 Oct 2026): AWS Direct Connect pay-as-you-go and flat-rate pricing (from 15 Sep 2026), Azure ExpressRoute and Data Box from the Azure retail prices API, Google Cloud Interconnect list prices. Cross-connect, EU-side line and lead time are estimates. A private line only fits a move to your own data centre or colocation rack: Hetzner, Scaleway and most EU clouds sell no direct-connect product (OVHcloud Connect is the exception), so for those the internet route applies. The free-egress-for-leaving programmes of all three clouds cover internet transfers only.
  • Snowflake credit prices and transfer fees are from Snowflake's Service Consumption Table (effective 15 Oct 2026), on-demand rates. Unloading to a bucket in the same region has no Snowflake transfer fee. Unload speed is not published: the default (about 0.1 TB per credit) is an estimate, so time a sample unload and enter your own.
  • Where a provider has no managed product (for example, a managed database on Hetzner), we price a self-hosted equivalent on VMs and say so.
  • Prices last checked . Rows marked unverified could not be confirmed on the official price page. Always check before you commit.
